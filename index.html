FAQ

Before you come in.

What is semi private training?

A 60 minute session with one coach and a maximum of 3 clients. You get coached on your own plan, with the energy of a small group, at a lower price than private training.

Is the first session really free?

Yes. Your first small group session is free and you pay nothing to book it. After the session, if you want to continue, Romy helps you pick a package.

When can I do the free trial?

Free trials run on weekday mornings at 7, 8, 9 and 10 AM. You can book up to 7 days ahead, at least 12 hours before the session.

None of these times work? Leave your number or WhatsApp us and we find one with you.

I have an injury or pain. Can I still train?

Yes, that is what we are built for. Our physiotherapist designs and checks the programs, and your coach plans the session around your injury. Tell us about it when you book. If you need treatment first, we also offer physiotherapy sessions.

How much does it cost?

Small group 1:3: $140 for 8 sessions a month or $200 for 12 sessions a month, per person. Monthly packages are valid for 30 days.

Duo, private, physiotherapy, prehab, osteopathy and maderotherapy prices are in the Wellbe app, or ask Romy on WhatsApp. You pay at the center, cash or Whish.
